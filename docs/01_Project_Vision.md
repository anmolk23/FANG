# 🎵 FANG 

Version: 0.1

Author: Anmol Kumar

---

# Vision

To become an intelligent music management platform where users can organize, manage and discover their favourite music across every streaming service from one place.

Instead of replacing Spotify, YouTube Music or other platforms, FANG connects them and provides one unified experience.

---

# Mission

Enable music lovers to access, organize and manage their favourite songs without worrying about which platform they are stored on.

---

# Why FANG Exists

Millions of users today listen to music across multiple streaming platforms.

A song may exist on YouTube Music but not Spotify.

Another song may only be available on Spotify.

As a result, users maintain multiple playlists, multiple liked song collections and multiple music libraries.

There is currently no single platform where users can connect all of their streaming accounts and manage their complete music collection.

FANG solves this problem.

---

# The Problem

Current music streaming platforms are excellent at streaming music.

However, they work as isolated ecosystems.

Users who use multiple platforms face problems such as:

• Different liked songs on different apps

• Multiple playlists across services

• Difficulty remembering where a song was saved

• Constant switching between applications

• No unified music library

---

# The Solution

FANG allows users to connect multiple music streaming accounts into one application.

Users can:

• Connect Spotify

• Connect YouTube Music

• Connect Apple Music

• Connect other supported services

After connecting their accounts, users can:

• View all liked songs in one place

• Organize playlists

• Search across platforms

• Discover music

• Generate AI playlists

• Open songs directly in their preferred streaming service
